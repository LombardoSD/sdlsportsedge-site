Research notice This week's predictions come from an experimental research model, tracked live for transparency. 2025 results are a retrospective replay, not a live record: each week was scored from earlier games only, then graded against the closing line. 2026 picks are made before kickoff, from a model still running on a smaller set of inputs than prior seasons had — recent quarterback and offensive-line form, pass-rush pressure, and explosive-play rates are being rebuilt for the current season game by game. Early 2026 confidence numbers should be read with that in mind. Moneyline probabilities also lean mainly on the betting market's own price, since testing showed our model's own estimates were overconfident on their own and didn't improve on the market by themselves.
