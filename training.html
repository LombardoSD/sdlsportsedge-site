Real, out-of-sample accuracy and log loss per week, graded against final results. Each row uses that week's stored pregame picks — nothing is recomputed with hindsight.
Research notice This week's predictions come from an experimental research model, tracked live for transparency. 2025 results are a retrospective reconstruction, not a live record: the model was trained on the 2025 games and shown each game's closing lines, so they overstate what it can do in real time. 2026 picks are made before kickoff, from a model still running on a smaller set of inputs than prior seasons had — recent quarterback and offensive-line form, pass-rush pressure, and explosive-play rates are being rebuilt for the current season game by game. Early 2026 confidence numbers should be read with that in mind. Moneyline probabilities also lean mainly on the betting market's own price, since testing showed our model's own estimates were overconfident on their own and didn't improve on the market by themselves.
